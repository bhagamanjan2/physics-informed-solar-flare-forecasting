1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This project develops an operational early-warning system forecasting significant
solar flares (GOES C, M, and X classes in both absolute flux and background-subtracted
fclass) 30 minutes in advance. The models are evaluated on a strict, chronological,
unseen held-out test set spanning April 4 to December 31, 2024 (300,019 minutes).

Under the primary skill metric — True Skill Statistic (TSS = Recall - POFD) — models
are evaluated symmetrically on rare flare detection and quiet background rejection.
Thresholds are strictly tuned on validation data to maximize TSS.

3. KEY RESEARCH & ENGINEERING FINDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Best Performing Model: Logistic Regression (Balanced) (lr)
   • Selected via the locked primary selection rule: Highest mean TSS across
     blocked cross-validation folds (CV Mean TSS = 0.2859 ± 0.0576).
   • Test TSS: 0.2598 [95% Bootstrap CI: 0.2279 – 0.2898]
   • Test HSS: 0.1876 [95% Bootstrap CI: 0.1628 – 0.2125]
   • Recall (POD): 68.99% [95% CI: 64.37% – 73.43%] (48,227 positive flare minutes caught).
   • False Alarm Ratio (FAR): 67.24% [95% CI: 65.44% – 69.20%].
   • Parsimonious 34-parameter linear formulation provides complete interpretability,
     fast convex training, and provable stability without tree-based overfitting risks.

2. Feature Ablation Insights:
   • G1 (24h/6h Flare History) is the single most decisive clue group, adding
     +0.072 ΔTSS and +0.039 ΔHSS over raw flux brightness alone (p < 0.01).
   • G2 (STFT Oscillations) and G3 (Annual Phase) do not provide statistically
     significant predictive gains within 95% bootstrap confidence intervals.

3. Methodological Safeguards:
   • Chronological partitioning (60% Train / 15% Val / 25% Test) prevents
     temporal look-ahead contamination across Solar Cycle 25.
   • Dual-class gate (abs ∈ {C,M,X} AND fclass ∈ {C,M,X}) eliminates 85% of spurious
     micro-bumps caused by elevated solar maximum backgrounds.
